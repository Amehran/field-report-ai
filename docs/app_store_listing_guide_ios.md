### Full Description
Field Report AI is the ultimate site inspection and daily construction log tool for contractors, engineers, project managers, and field inspectors. 

Simply dictate site observations, contractor activities, weather conditions, and safety items. Our advanced AI instantly converts your voice into structured, executive-level field inspection reports and exports professional PDF documents with single-tap sharing.

KEY FEATURES:
- **Instant AI Voice Dictation:** Record site notes hands-free on-site; AI formats observations into clear, professional prose.
- **Instant PDF Export:** Generate branded PDF inspection reports ready for clients, superintendents, and stakeholders.
- **Offline Kotlin Multiplatform Engine:** Work seamlessly on job sites with low connectivity; data syncs automatically when online.
- **Multi-Store Licensing & StoreKit 2:** Choose between flexible Monthly, Annual (25% off), or Lifetime License passes.
- **Custom Branding:** Save default company info, contractor names, and inspector details.

PRIVACY & SECURITY:
