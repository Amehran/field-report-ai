## 2. Full Description (max 4000 chars)

```text
Field Report AI is the fastest, easiest way for contractors, field service technicians, and independent trade professionals to document completed work on-site and send professional PDF reports to customers.

Stop spending your evenings organizing site photos, typing out long text updates, or struggling with complicated desktop software. With Field Report AI, you can complete and share a polished service report right from your Android phone before leaving the job site—in under 3 minutes.

Key Features:

📸 BEFORE & AFTER PHOTO CAPTURE
Attach clear, high-resolution site photos. Automatically tag photos as "Before", "After", or inspection points to show clients exactly what work was performed.

🎙️ VOICE-TO-REPORT DICTATION
Simply speak into your phone at the end of a job. Describe the work completed, parts replaced, or site conditions. Our AI model transcribes and structures your dictation into clear, professional customer summaries.

📄 INSTANT PDF REPORT GENERATION
Generate beautiful, business-grade PDF job reports instantly. Customizable headers automatically format your business name, technician credentials, job title, customer details, and structured itemized lists.

⚡ ZERO BULLET/RAW NOTE MESS
Our intelligent report engine removes ugly bullet points and raw technician notes, converting them into clean "ISSUE:" and "SERVICE:" statements that build client trust.

📱 EASY SHARING VIA WHATSAPP & EMAIL
Share generated PDF reports with clients via WhatsApp, Email, or SMS directly from the app. Keep customer communications organized and transparent.

🔐 SECURE CLOUD BACKUP
All your reports and media are automatically synced to secure cloud storage, ensuring you never lose job documentation history.

Who is Field Report AI for?
• HVAC Technicians & Electricians
• Plumbers & General Contractors
• Site Inspectors & Facility Managers
• Appliance Repair Specialists & Field Technicians

Download Field Report AI today and transform the way you document field work!
```
