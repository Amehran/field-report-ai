# Privacy Policy for Field Report AI

**Effective Date:** September 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 20, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Field Report AI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Android mobile application ("Field Report AI") and related services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information You Provide
- **Account Data:** Email address, user name, business name, and account credentials when you register via Firebase Authentication.
- **Report Content:** Customer names, job titles, service addresses, typed notes, voice audio recordings, and photos captured or uploaded for job reports.

### B. Device Permissions
- **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** Used solely to capture voice notes when you initiate a voice report dictation.
- **Camera (`CAMERA`):** Used solely to capture site photos when you take "Before" or "After" job photos inside the app.
- **Storage Access:** Used to save generated PDF field reports to your local device storage.

### C. Automatically Collected Technical Data
- Device model, operating system version, crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics, and app usage metrics.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- **AI Processing:** Voice audio dictations and typed notes are processed by our secure AI service solely to generate structured report summaries (Customer, ISSUE, SERVICE). Your data is not sold or used for training public models.
- **Report Cloud Sync & Storage:** Job reports, photos, and generated PDFs are securely backed up to Firebase Firestore and Cloud Storage under your authenticated account.
- **Service Operations:** To authenticate users, troubleshoot crashes, and provide customer support.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services
We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with necessary infrastructure providers:
- **Google Firebase:** For authentication, database storage, file hosting, and crash logging.
- **AI Processing Services:** For speech-to-text transcription and text structuring.

## 5. Security and Data Retention
We implement industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (Firebase security rules). Your reports remain stored in your account until you delete them or request account deletion.

## 6. User Rights & Account Deletion
You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated field reports and uploaded media at any time by contacting our support team at `support@fieldreport.ai`.
