# Privacy Policy for Field Report AI

**Effective Date:** September 22, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 22, 2026  

**Field Report AI** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application **Field Report AI** (the "Application").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the Application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when using the Application to generate field inspection reports:

### A. Personal Account Data
- **Account Credentials:** Email address and user ID collected via Firebase Authentication for account creation, login, and cross-device sync.

### B. User Content & Field Media
- **Audio Recordings:** Recorded voice notes captured on site to transcribe dictation into structured report sections.
- **Photos & Images:** Job site photos uploaded to attach equipment damage, inspection evidence, or work completed.
- **Report Notes & Business Details:** Customer names, job titles, addresses, technician notes, pricing details, company logos, and digital signatures embedded into exported PDF reports.

### C. Device & Technical Information
- **Device Identifiers:** Hardware device ID hashes collected strictly for trial export validation and anti-tampering protection.
- **App Usage & Diagnostic Data:** Crash logs and performance metrics captured to ensure reliability and app performance.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- **Core Functionality:** To process voice notes, analyze site photos, format PDF reports, and enable cloud storage and sync.
- **Account Management:** To authenticate users, manage subscription entitlements, and process in-app purchases via Google Play Billing.
- **App Improvement:** To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and optimize report generation speed.
- **Security & Compliance:** To prevent fraud, enforce trial limits, and comply with legal requirements.

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- **Encryption:** All data transmitted between the Application and cloud servers is encrypted using industry-standard **HTTPS / TLS 1.3**.
- **Data at Rest:** Photos, audio, and report data stored in Firebase Cloud Storage and Firestore are protected with server-side encryption and strict access rules.
- **Offline Storage:** Reports and draft media cached locally on your device are stored within the Application's private internal storage directory.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

### Your Data Rights
You have the right to access, export, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time.

### Requesting Data Deletion
To request full deletion of your account and all associated field reports, photos, and voice data:
- **Email Request:** Send an email to **support@fieldreport.ai** with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and your account email.
- **Processing Time:** All associated account data and cloud-stored media will be permanently purged within 30 days of request confirmation.
